# Note: cinematic hero

Source: https://styles.refero.design/style/21cfe0c1-778d-4613-9f47-a5718eb929b3 (Refero Styles entry for Auros).
Summary in our words.

## What the system does
- Dark teal canvas (`#012624`, `#011d1c`, `#003734`) with a teal-to-pink gradient reserved for one button.
- Hero: a rotating 3D particle sphere of small dots behind a centered text stack; oversized kinetic type
  (86..295px) as an environmental element.
- One geometric grotesk at weight 500 for all headings; negative tracking at scale (-0.04em at 61px,
  -0.046em at 86px), line-height 1.0 above 36px, 1.4 for body; wide positive tracking on small uppercase labels.
- Radii 6px (buttons) and 16px (cards); depth from surface colour steps, no shadows.

## Taken
The rotating particle sphere as the hero idea, and the display scale: medium weight, -0.04em tracking,
line-height under 1 at display size, kinetic sizes for one section marker.

## Changed (rejected)
- The whole teal palette and both gradients: our ground is mist/ink and colour only means a decision.
- 6px/16px radii: we keep 0px everywhere.
- Centered hero stack: our hero text is left-aligned on the grid, the sphere sits right.
- Decorative particles: in our scene each particle is a request, and it only takes a colour when the gate decides it
  (lamp colours as emissive light), so the sphere carries information.
- Uppercase tracked eyebrows: labels are sentence-case mono and carry data (rule ids, hashes, field names).
